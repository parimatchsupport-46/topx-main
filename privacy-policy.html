Privacy Policy

Information about privacy and personal data.

Draft for review: This page is a starting point, not a final privacy policy. The operator must confirm its actual data practices, contact details and applicable legal requirements before publishing it as a policy.

1. Information to review

Depending on how support is provided, information may include:

  • Contact and account information you choose to provide.
  • Details of your support request, including transaction references or screenshots.
  • Technical information generated by the website or its service providers, if collected.

2. How information may be used

  • To understand and respond to support requests.
  • To investigate account or transaction issues and prevent misuse.
  • To maintain the website and comply with applicable obligations, where relevant.

The operator should document the actual purposes and legal bases that apply.

3. Sharing and retention

The operator should identify any service providers or other recipients of personal data, explain why information is shared, and specify how long each category is retained or how retention is determined.

4. Security and your choices

  • Do not send passwords, one-time codes, PINs or full card details in a support request.
  • The operator should explain its security measures and any rights to access, correct or delete personal information under applicable law.
  • For a privacy request, use the official support contact once it is provided by the operator.

5. Cookies and updates

This site stores your light or dark theme preference in your browser’s local storage. The operator should disclose any additional cookies or analytics actually used and how preferences can be managed. This draft should be updated when practices change.
