Frequently Asked Questions

Find step-by-step guidance for common issues.

1. How do I get help?

  1. Go to the home page and choose the option matching your issue.
  2. Prepare a brief description and any relevant reference number.
  3. Open the complaint page using Get Help. Form fields and support submission are not yet available.

2. My deposit has not appeared. What should I check?

  1. Confirm the payment status in your payment account.
  2. Check that you used the correct account and payment details.
  3. Keep the amount, date and transaction reference ready for support. Do not share passwords or one-time codes.

3. What if my withdrawal is pending or failed?

  1. Check the withdrawal status and the details you entered.
  2. Review any messages or instructions shown in your account.
  3. If the issue continues, prepare the withdrawal reference and contact official support when available.

4. How can I resolve an email verification issue?

  1. Confirm that your email address is correct.
  2. Check your inbox, spam and junk folders for the verification email.
  3. Request a new verification email if the service offers that option. Contact official support if the problem continues.

5. How do I start a live chat?

The Live Chat option opens a complaint page; a live chat service has not been connected yet.

6. What information should I never share?

Do not share your password, one-time verification codes, PIN or full payment card details. Provide only the information needed to explain your issue through an official channel.
