Cookie Policy

Learn about browser storage and how to manage it.

Draft for review: The operator should confirm all cookies and third-party technologies used, applicable consent requirements and contact details before publishing a final policy.

1. What are cookies and local storage?

Cookies are small pieces of data stored by your browser. Local storage is another browser feature that can save information on your device. Both can help a website remember preferences.

2. What does this website store?

The current website code uses browser local storage to remember your light or dark theme preference under the key theme. This helps keep your chosen appearance when you return.

The current page code does not set cookies directly. The operator should verify whether hosting providers or any future services use cookies or similar technologies.

3. How to manage your preference

  1. Use the theme button on the website to change between light and dark mode.
  2. To remove the saved preference, open your browser’s site settings for this website.
  3. Clear the site’s stored data or local storage. This may also remove other site data stored by your browser.

If storage is blocked or cleared, your browser or device appearance preference may be used instead.

4. Third parties and changes

If analytics, live chat or other external services are added, the operator should explain their technologies, purposes, retention and any consent choices here. This policy should be updated as the website changes.
